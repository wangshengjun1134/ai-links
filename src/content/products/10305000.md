## 【产品概述】
Gobble Bot是一款 AI 驱动的智能工具。You have a website, PDF or a Youtube video and want to make a GPT chatbot on ChatGPT. Simply use Gobble Bot to turn it all into one text file, ready to use for training.
What is this?
Gobble Bot is an all-in-1 scraper designed to convert all different kinds of content into one text file.
Why do I need it?
You need it if you want to create a GPT (a custom ChatGPT chatbot) but your content is a website, YouTube video or spans more than 20 files (the current upload limit for GPTs). You can crawl the whole internet and turn it into a chatbot, I guess.
What data does it handle?
Youtube videos with transciptions, public websites and files with extensions: .TXT, .MD, .XSL, .PDF, .DOC, .DOCX, .ODT, .OTT, .RTF, .HTML, .HTM, .ATOM, .RSS, .XML, .XLS, .XLSX, .XLSB, .XLSM, .XLTX, .CSV, .ODS, .OTS, .PPT
