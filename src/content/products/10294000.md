Letter from the founders
Alice wasn't supposed to be a product. It was just us, frustrated, hitting Cmd+Tab for the hundredth time that day to reach ChatGPT.
We're productivity freaks who've spent years building systems that run our businesses on autopilot. When LLMs arrived, we had a realization: what if our automations could be triggered by natural conversation instead of complex workflows?
So we built Alice for ourselves. A native AI layer that lived in our OS, summoned instantly, connected to everything. No more tab-switching. No more copy-paste marathons.
